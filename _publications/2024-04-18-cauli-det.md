![Cauli-Det architecture](/images/cauli-det.jpg)

Designed and trained a modified YOLOv8 architecture for multi-class cauliflower disease detection and localization from smartphone-captured field images. Precision 93.2%, recall 82.6%, mAP 91.1% across three disease classes (Bacterial Soft Rot, Downy Mildew, Black Rot) on a 656-image dataset.

Code: [github.com/manchitro/cauli-det](https://github.com/manchitro/cauli-det)
